Haiti Poverty

• Historical Independence Debt: After gaining independence in 1804, Haiti was forced to pay massive financial reparations to France, draining its treasury for over a century through high-interest foreign loans.

• Political Instability & Violence: Decades of weak governance, foreign interventions, and a power vacuum have allowed armed gangs to control major territories, disrupt supply chains, and cripple local commerce.

• Natural Disasters: Devastating earthquakes (such as the 2010 disaster) and recurring hurricanes (like Hurricane Matthew) have repeatedly destroyed housing, agriculture, and public health systems.

• Agricultural & Infrastructure Deficits: Over half the labor force relies on agriculture, but productivity remains very low due to a lack of modern technology, roads, irrigation, and credit access.

Life in Haiti

• Gang Control: Armed gangs control large portions of downtown Port-au-Prince and major transit routes, leading to widespread violence, kidnappings, and roadblocks.

• Displacement: Prolonged violence has internally displaced hundreds of thousands of people across the country.

• Political Vacuum: The country has faced an extended lack of elected officials and central government breakdown following the 2021 assassination of President Jovenel Moïse..
